How to Play

Fill in the empty cells with numbers from 1 to 9.

Each row, column, and 3x3 block must contain all numbers from 1 to 9 without repetition.

Use the "Solve" button to automatically solve the puzzle.

Use the "Clear" button to reset the puzzle.

Use the "Hint" button to get help with a cell.

Keep an eye on the timer and score to track your progress!
